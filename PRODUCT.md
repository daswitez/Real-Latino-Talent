# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
U.S. business owners, founders, entrepreneurs, established freelancers, and small-to-medium-sized business (SMB) managers who need high-quality talent but find the U.S. labor market cost-prohibitive. They value reliability, communication, and quality over cheap volume.

## Product Purpose
Real Latino Talent (RLT) connects U.S. companies with pre-vetted, highly qualified, bilingual Latin American remote professionals. It provides "talent + support + management", bridging the cultural and operational gap between remote workers and internal teams so that remote talent feels truly *in-house*.

## Brand Personality
- Professional yet approachable
- Clear and transparent
- Human-centered (warm, empathetic)
- Solution-oriented
- Core Color: PANTONE 2935 C (#0055B8)

## Anti-references
- Cheap outsourcing agencies competing solely on low cost ("staff of underpaid offshore resources")
- Cold, corporate, transaction-heavy staffing interfaces
- Generic stock office imagery

## Design Principles
- **Quality over Volume**: Focus on clean, high-contrast, premium layouts that justify the investment.
- **In-house Integration**: Design flows and copy that evoke closeness, trust, and alignment.
- **Support-first**: Ensure the ongoing support/management differentiators are visually elevated over standard placement steps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratios must adhere to WCAG 2.1 AA (≥4.5:1 for body text, ≥3:1 for large display elements).
- Do not overlay white or light gray text on bright colors like Lime (#CDDE00) or light blue.
- Provide smooth, hardware-accelerated motion that respects prefers-reduced-motion.
