# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
U.S. SMBs (Small & Medium Businesses), entrepreneurs, and established freelancers who need reliable, high-quality remote talent but cannot afford U.S.-based costs. They value quality, communication, and long-term partnership over finding the cheapest option.

## Product Purpose
Real Latino Talent (RLT) connects U.S. companies with bilingual Latin American professionals. The goal is to provide a comprehensive solution—talent + support + management—so the remote team feels like a true in-house extension, reducing the typical friction of offshore hiring.

## Brand Personality
Professional yet approachable. Clear and transparent. Human-centered (not cold corporate). Solution-oriented. It evokes trust, empathy, and high standards.

## Anti-references
- Transactional "recruiting only" agencies.
- "Cheap offshore labor" positioning or messaging.
- Cold, corporate, overly technical or distant aesthetics.
- Generic, overly rounded (32px+ radius), or purely decorative AI-generated "SaaS template" layouts.

## Design Principles
- **Partnership Over Placement**: Emphasize that RLT stays involved after hiring. Design should highlight "Ongoing Support" as the main differentiator.
- **Human Connection**: Use imagery and layouts that feel authentic, close, and reliable, avoiding sterile tech-bro vibes.
- **Premium Quality**: Avoid looking like a budget agency. The use of typography (Nunito Sans), controlled color palette, and layout should project competence and high-end service.
- **Clear & Direct Messaging**: Remove jargon. Use hierarchy to guide the user naturally through the problem-solution narrative.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure WCAG AA contrast compliance, particularly when combining the brand's lime (`#CDDE00`) and navy (`#08223D`) colors. Readability is paramount for U.S. business owners reviewing the site.
